# Tutorial: use the touchpad as a mouse, a stick or a menu

DualShock 4 and DualSense controllers have a touchpad you can also press like a big button. You'll make it a trackpad, then split it so each half does a different job. Open a profile's **Touchpad** tab.

**You'll need** a controller with a touchpad, or `padwight debug`, which has one you drag on with the mouse.

## 1. A trackpad

![The Touchpad tab: the mode set to Mouse pointer, speed, the three extras and the click](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marcinkoza0922/padwight/main/docs/images/manual/touchpad-panel.png "The pad starts out as a mouse.")

1. Choose **Mouse pointer** for the whole pad (1). **Speed** (2) is how far the pointer moves when your finger crosses the pad.
2. Tick the extras (3):
   - **Tap to click**: a quick tap clicks, so you needn't press the pad down.
   - **Two-finger scroll**: two fingers moving together scroll.
   - **Keep gliding after a flick**: the pointer keeps sliding after you flick and lift, like a trackball, until you touch the pad.
3. **Click** (4) is what pressing the pad down does. It takes any action, like a button.

## 2. Split it in two

Tick **Split into a left and a right half, each with its own job**. Each half now has its own box and click. A finger belongs to the half it landed on, so both thumbs can work at once.

A common layout: the left half a **Gamepad stick** for moving (tick **Center where the finger lands**, so you needn't aim for the middle), and the right half a **Mouse pointer**.

## 3. A menu on the pad

Choose **Menu zones** to divide the pad into the items of one of your [menus](Tutorial-Menus). While a finger is on the pad, the menu shows with the item under your finger picked. **Choose by** sets how you run it: press the pad down, or lift your finger. Untick **Show the menu while a finger is on the pad** once you know the zones by heart.

## 4. Swipes

**Swipes** runs an action for a quick stroke up, down, left or right. **Swipe length** is how far the stroke must go.

## 5. Check it

The **Visualizers** panel under the boxes draws the pad: each half's job, the menu's items, and your fingers.

Tick **Profile requires touchpad**, at the top of the tab, only if the profile depends on the pad. Players without one then aren't offered the profile.
