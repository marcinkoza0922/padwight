# Layers

A layer is a second set of mappings that applies while you hold a button: hold LB and the face buttons type F1 to F4, or hold the aim button and the mouse stick slows down. Anything the layer doesn't change keeps its normal mapping.

## Holding a layer

Choose **Hold layer…** as the action of any button, trigger, stick direction, zone, gesture or combo. The layer is on while you hold that input.

A layer can't be toggled on, and a menu item can't hold one. Mappings that should stay on until you switch back belong in a [mode](modes.md). Layers apply on top of whichever mode is on.

## Several at once

Layers can stack. If two of them disagree about a button, the one turned on most recently wins.

## Showing a layer

While it's on, a layer can show its name, one of the setup's info overlays, a generated sheet of its bindings, or nothing.

The [Guide overlay](guide-button.md)'s Mappings give each layer a button holds a section of its own.

## Editing

Layers are edited on the setup's **Layers** tab. A setting the layer doesn't change reads "Same as" the profile shown under **Shown over**; **Override** gives the layer its own value, and **Back to base** removes it.
