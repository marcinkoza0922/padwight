# The Guide button

The Guide button (the PlayStation, Xbox or Home button) works the same in every game. Padwight keeps it from the game and from Steam.

## The Guide overlay

Tap Guide to open the overlay, and again to close it. It opens when you let go, so Guide held with another button is a shortcut instead.

The overlay has four parts:

- **Notes** on the left: what the setup's author wants you to know, such as when to switch modes.
- **The menu** in the middle: Quick Settings, Edit Controls, the on-screen keyboard and numpad, mouse mode, media controls, Switch Profile (to pick or make another profile) and, while Steam runs, the Steam overlay. Move with the D-pad or Left Stick, choose with A, close with B.
- **Mappings** on the right: what each button, trigger and stick does in the current profile and [mode](modes.md), written by the author on the profile's **Guide** tab. Each [layer](layers.md) a button holds gets its own section below. When the setup has modes, press Left or Right to see the others; the heading names the mode shown. The Right Stick turns the pages of a long list.
- **Shortcuts** in the bottom left corner: the Guide shortcuts below.

## Shortcuts

Hold Guide and press one of these, or press it alone with the overlay open:

- **X**: on-screen keyboard.
- **Y**: on-screen numpad.
- **LB**: media controls.
- **RB**: take a screenshot.
- **L3**: start or stop a screen recording.
- **Start**: the in-game menu, with Quick Settings and Edit Controls (see [Changing controls while you play](in-game-menu.md)).
- **Hold Select for 2 seconds**: force quit the focused window.

Guide also closes anything else on screen: the on-screen keyboard or numpad, the media controls, a menu, the in-game menu (asking first if you have unsaved changes), or the offer to add a game.

Anything else pressed with Guide held does nothing, so it never reaches the game. These shortcuts are the same in every game; move them on **App settings › Controller shortcuts** (below), or bind the same actions to other buttons on a profile's **Buttons** tab.

## Mouse mode

Hold Guide and press the Right Stick (or press it with the overlay open) for mouse mode. The bottom left corner lists the controls:

- **Right Stick**: moves the mouse.
- **Left Stick**: scrolls. Pressing it clicks the middle button.
- **RT / LT**: left click / right click.
- **LB / RB**: back / forward.
- **D-pad Right, Down, Left**: Enter, Tab and Escape.
- **D-pad Up**: holds Shift, so a click or key with it is shifted.
- **X**: holds Ctrl. **Y**: holds Alt. **B**: Delete.

Press Guide to leave; your profile and [mode](modes.md) are as you left them. Other buttons do nothing in mouse mode, except those that switch modes. A button set to **Switch mode…** "Mouse mode" turns it on too.

## Moving the shortcuts

On **App settings**, the **Controller shortcuts** tab sets these for every setup. Each shortcut sits on one of:

- **Button**: a single button.
- **Double tap**, **Triple tap** or **Long press**: a gesture of a single button.
- **Combo**: two to four buttons pressed together, such as Start + Select.
- **Off**: nothing. Only switching off the overlay's opener warns you.

**Set by pressing** picks a button by pressing it on the controller. **Reset** puts a shortcut back, and **Put every shortcut back** at the bottom resets them all.

### What opens the Guide overlay

The Guide button, unless you move it. A controller without one can use, say, Start + Select as a combo, or a long press of Select.

- As a **button**, it works as Guide does: tap to open or close the overlay, or hold it and press a shortcut. The game no longer gets that button.
- As a **gesture** or **combo**, it opens and closes the overlay, and the shortcuts are pressed with the overlay open.
- Either way, it also leaves mouse mode.

The Guide button is reserved for this: no other shortcut can sit on it, and a profile can't map it. With the opener moved elsewhere, Guide does nothing.

Setting the opener to **Off** is allowed, with a warning: without it, the overlay, the Guide shortcuts and mouse mode can't be reached.

If a profile already uses the input you chose, it would do both. The tab lists those profiles, and a ⚠ marks their setups in the sidebar. Change the opener or the profile's mapping.

### The Guide shortcuts and mouse mode's keys

The tab lists the Guide shortcuts (keyboard, numpad, media controls, Quick Settings, screenshot, recording, force quit and mouse mode) and mouse mode's keys (Shift, Enter, Tab, Escape, Ctrl, Alt, Delete, middle click, back and forward). Mouse mode's sticks and triggers can't be moved.

When two shortcuts share an input, or one shares the opener's, a ⚠ marks both rows. They still save, but only one can work. A combo clashes with its own buttons used alone, and with a combo that shares them.

The keyboard, numpad and media controls close with the opener plus their own shortcut, wherever you moved it, or with the opener alone.

Hints on screen that say "press Guide" show your opener instead, as does `{guide}` in your own info overlays.

### Confirm and back

**Menu buttons** sets the buttons that choose and go back (A and B by default) in the Guide overlay's menu, the keyboard and numpad, the media controls, the in-game menu, the library offer, and list, grid and carousel menus. Hints on screen follow.

Only buttons those panels don't otherwise use are offered: A, B, Select, the stick clicks and the back paddles. The button you pick trades places with A (or B). Confirm on B and Back on A swaps the two, as on a Nintendo controller.

A menu with its own close button keeps it, and directional and button menus aren't changed. The tab warns when Confirm and Back share a button, or one sits on a Guide shortcut.

### The panels' own buttons

The last groups, **Media controls buttons**, **On-screen numpad buttons** and **On-screen keyboard buttons**, move the buttons those panels use, such as play or pause, Enter, Backspace and close. A ⚠ marks one that clashes with the opener.

## Force quit

Holding Select for 2 seconds closes the program in the focused window: it's asked to close, and stopped if it hasn't after 3 seconds. Letting go early cancels. The desktop and Padwight are never closed.

## Screenshots and recordings

Screenshots go to `Pictures/Screenshots` and recordings to `Videos/Recordings`, each in a folder for the game. Recording needs gpu-screen-recorder; without it, a message says so.

## Steam

Open Steam's overlay from the Guide menu, while Steam is running. Guide itself never reaches Steam.
