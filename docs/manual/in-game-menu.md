# Changing controls while you play

Hold Guide and press Start to open the in-game menu over any game (the shortcut can be moved; see [The Guide button](guide-button.md)). Move with the Left Stick or D-pad, press A to choose, and B to go back or close it.

## Quick Settings

- stick speed, for sticks that move the mouse, and gyro sensitivity, when gyro moves it. A layer or mode with its own speed (an aim layer, say) gets its own row.
- gyro on or off
- Invert Y, which flips every up-and-down setting in the profile
- stick deadzones
- rumble strength, for every game
- overlay size, for the keyboard, numpad, media controls and menus
- Nintendo button layout, for this setup
- profile, to switch to another of the setup's profiles
- **Turn remapping off**, a way out if a mapping gets in the way: press A twice, and the controller goes straight to the game until you turn remapping back on from the tray or the settings window

Left and right change a number by about 10% (with RB held, by half), repeating while held. A changed number shows what it was when you opened the menu. A flips a switch or picks the next profile.

## Switch Profile

**Switch Profile** on the Guide menu lists the profiles of the game in front of you (or General's). The one you're on says "(current)", and one your controller can't play says what it needs, such as "(needs gyro)". Pick one to switch to it.

**New profile…** at the bottom makes a profile and switches to it: a copy of the current one, a template, or in a game, a copy of one of General's. It's saved only when you save.

## Edit Controls

The full editor for the current profile and game. Its first page lists **Buttons** (with back paddles, when the controller has them), **Sticks**, **Triggers**, **Gyro**, **Touchpad**, **Combos**, **Modes**, **Layers**, **Macros**, **Menus**, **Info overlays**, **Log overlays**, the **Guide overlay**, the **Profile** and the **Game**. Almost anything the settings window does for a game can be done here. Changes apply at once, and are saved only when you save.

- Left and right change a row's value. A chooses; B goes back a page.
- Text is typed on the on-screen keyboard, whose keys then go into the field instead of the game. Start sets the text; hold B to cancel.
- Pages about a stick, trigger, gyro or the touchpad show pictures that follow your controller. A menu or overlay being edited is drawn where it goes.
- Deleting asks you to press A again. LB and RB page through a long list.

The wiki's *Editing controls in game* page lists every page of the editor.

Leaving with unsaved changes asks first: **A** saves, **X** discards, and **B** keeps editing.

## When no setup matches the window

If no setup matches the window in front, your first change creates one for it, named after the program (`eldenring` for `eldenring.exe`) and copied from General's active profile. General stays as it was.

> **Tip:** the top line of the menu shows where you are, such as `Menu › Edit Controls › A button`.
